# Flockle Support

Need help with Flockle, the daily social prediction game? We're happy to help.

## Contact us
Email **[andyliang810@gmail.com](mailto:andyliang810@gmail.com)** and we'll get
back to you as soon as we can. Please include your username and a short
description of the issue so we can help faster.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I play?
Each day you answer a prompt, then guess how everyone else answered. You earn
points for matching the most popular responses, and you build a streak by playing
every day.

### How do I add friends?
Open the **Friends** tab and add other players by username. Once you're friends,
you can see each other's answers (after a round ends) and scores.

### How do I report or block another player?
Tap an answer or a player to **report** content that breaks the rules, or
**block** a player so you no longer see them. We review every report and act on
violations, typically within 24 hours.

### How do I delete my account?
In the app, go to **Profile → Delete account**. This permanently deletes your
account, answers, scores, streak, and friends, and cannot be undone.
