# Revel Privacy Policy

_Effective date: July 31, 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how Andy Liang ("REVEL," "we," "us")
collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information when you use the
REVEL app, website, and related services (the "Service"). It is part of your
agreement with us under the [Terms of Service].

We wrote this to be readable. If anything is unclear, contact us at
cookedevents123@gmail.com.

## 1. The Short Version

- REVEL is built around real-world events, so location is central to the
  product. Precise location is collected only with your permission, and you
  control when it's on and what others see.
- When you check in to an event, other people at that event (and the host) can
  see that you're there. That's the point of the feature — but it's your choice
  to check in.
- Your Flash and 33h posts are designed to disappear, and we delete them on
  schedule — but we can't stop other people from screenshotting them first.
- We do not sell your personal information, and we do not currently use it for
  cross-context behavioral advertising.
- Hosts get limited attendee information to run their events, under contractual
  restrictions — they can't market to you without your separate opt-in.
- You can access, correct, download, or delete your data, and you must be 18 or
  older to use REVEL.

The rest of this policy is the detail behind those sentences.

## 2. Information We Collect

### a. Information you give us

- **Account and profile:** username, phone number,
  password, and your 8 profile photos and bio details you choose to add. We do
  not collect an email address for your account.
- **Content:** photos and posts (including Flash and 33h posts), captions,
  comments, ratings and reviews of events and hosts, and messages you send to
  matches.
- **Event activity you initiate:** events you RSVP to, events you check in to,
  Waves you send, and events you create or cohost.
- **Communications with us:** support requests, reports you file, survey
  responses.

### b. Information collected automatically

- **Location information:** with your permission, precise (GPS-level) location
  from your device. We use it for the features in Section 4. If you deny
  permission, we may still infer approximate location (city-level) from your IP
  address to show nearby events. Precise location is sensitive data and is
  collected only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in your
  device or app settings.
- **Usage information:** how you use the app — screens viewed, features used,
  events viewed, Waves sent and received, check-in times, content interactions.
- **Device and log information:** device model, operating system, app version,
  language, IP address, device identifiers, crash logs, and diagnostic data.
- **Approximate proximity signals:** to power Spontaneous Events, we process the
  locations of users who have location enabled to detect when several users are
  gathered in the same area (see Section 4(c)).

### c. Information from others

- **Other users:** content that depicts or mentions you (e.g., a photo taken at
  an event you attended), Waves sent to you, and invitations.
- **Hosts:** if a host adds you to a guest list or checks you in.
- **Service providers and partners:** delivery status from communication
  providers, and fraud or security signals.

We do not ask for or collect your contacts book unless you explicitly use a
contact-based friend-finding feature, in which case we will ask for permission
at that time and explain what we do with it.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

- **Provide the Service:** create your account, display your profile, show
  nearby and recommended events, process RSVPs and check-ins, deliver Waves,
  create matches, enable messaging between matches, and display your posts to
  the audiences you choose.
- **Personalize discovery:** rank and recommend events based on your activity,
  interests, ratings, connections, and (with permission) your location.
- **Power Spontaneous Events:** detect clusters of users in the same area and
  prompt those users about creating or joining a spontaneous gathering.
- **Keep the platform safe:** detect and prevent fraud, spam, fake events,
  abusive behavior, and violations of our Terms and Community Guidelines,
  including through automated analysis of content and activity; enforce age
  requirements; and protect users' rights and safety.
- **Communicate with you:** service messages, security alerts, check-in
  confirmations, event reminders and updates from events you joined, and — only
  with your opt-in — marketing messages, which you can stop at any time. Because
  we do not collect an email address, we reach you through in-app messages, push
  notifications, and text messages to the phone number on your account. Standard
  message and data rates may apply; reply STOP to any text to stop marketing
  texts.
- **Improve and develop the Service:** debugging, analytics, research on how
  features are used, and developing new features. Where possible we use
  aggregated or de-identified data for this.
- **Comply with law:** meet legal, tax, and regulatory obligations, respond to
  lawful requests, and establish or defend legal claims.

We do not use your personal information for automated decisions that produce
legal or similarly significant effects about you without human involvement.

## 4. Location: How It Actually Works

Because location is the heart of REVEL, the following describes how our location
features currently work:

- **(a) Discovery.** With location permission on, we use your location to show
  events near you, at the radius you configure. This use is visible only to you.
- **(b) Check-in and the event map.** When you check in to an event, your
  attendance becomes visible to other checked-in attendees and to the host, and
  you may appear on that event's attendee map. Friends may see which event you
  are at, according to your settings. Checking in is always an action you take —
  we never check you in automatically.
- **(c) Spontaneous Events.** If you have location enabled, our systems may
  detect that you and other users are gathered in the same area and prompt you
  about starting or joining a Spontaneous Event. Until you accept, other users
  are not shown your identity or location — they may only see aggregate signals
  (e.g., that activity exists in an area).
- **(d) What we don't do.** We do not broadcast your continuous real-time
  location to other users outside of the event contexts you opt into, and we do
  not sell location data.
- **(e) Controls.** You can turn precise location off at the device level at any
  time; you can leave (un-check-in from) an event; and you can control friend
  visibility in settings. Turning location off limits location-based features but
  does not affect the rest of the app.
- **(f) Retention.** Your last precise location point is retained until you turn off
  location tracking or on account deletion; check-in records (that you attended an event) are kept
  as part of your profile history until you delete them or your account.

## 5. How Information Is Shared

- **(a) With other users.** Your profile (username, photos, bio), your public
  posts, your ratings, your attendance at events you check in to, and — for
  matches — your messages. Your "Events Attended" history appears on your profile
  per your settings.
- **(b) With hosts and cohosts.** When you RSVP or check in, the event's host
  and cohosts can see your name/username, profile photo, RSVP status, and
  check-in status, so they can run the event. Under our Host &
  Organizer Agreement, hosts may use this only to operate the event, may not sell
  it, and may not market to you without your separate opt-in consent. Hosts are
  independent parties, and their own handling of information is also subject to
  applicable law and, where they have one, their own privacy policy.
- **(c) Service providers.** Companies that work for us under contract: cloud
  hosting, content delivery, analytics, customer support,
  communications (push/SMS/email), safety and moderation tools, and AI
  infrastructure providers. They may use your information only to provide
  services to us.
- **(d) Legal, safety, and rights.** We may disclose information when we believe
  in good faith it is required by law or legal process; or reasonably necessary
  to prevent fraud or abuse, protect the safety of any person, enforce our Terms,
  or protect our rights and property. Where the law allows and it would not
  create risk, we try to notify you of legal demands for your data.
- **(e) Business transfers.** If we are involved in a merger, acquisition,
  financing, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that
  transaction, subject to this policy's commitments.
- **(f) Aggregated or de-identified data.** We may share statistics that do not
  identify you (e.g., "1,200 people attended events in Blacksburg this weekend").
- **(g) With your consent.** Any other sharing happens only if you direct or
  agree to it.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share personal information
for cross-context behavioral advertising. If that ever changes, we will update
this policy first and provide the opt-outs required by law.

## 6. Ephemeral Content: What "Disappearing" Means

- **Flash posts** are removed from other users' feeds after they are viewed and
  do not appear on your profile. We delete them from our production systems
  within 1 day of expiry.
- **33h posts** are visible for 33 hours and then removed from the app and
  deleted from our production systems.
- **Backups.** Deleted content may persist in encrypted backups for up to
  90 days before being purged.
- **Safety and legal holds.** We may retain specific content beyond these
  windows where necessary to investigate violations, protect safety, or comply
  with a legal preservation obligation — in which case it is kept only as long as
  needed for that purpose and is not restored to the app.
- **Other people.** We cannot control screenshots or recordings made by people
  who saw your content while it was live. Treat every post accordingly.

## 7. How Long We Keep Information

- **Account and profile data:** for as long as your account exists.
- **Messages:** until you and your recipient deletes them, or your account is deleted.
- **Event history (RSVPs, check-ins):** until you delete individual records or
  your account.
- **Precise location points:** Until you turn off location tracking or your account is deleted (Section 4(f)).
- **Ephemeral content:** per Section 6.
- **Logs and diagnostics:** typically 12 months.

- **Marketing:** opt out of marketing texts by replying STOP, or turn them off

  sent.
